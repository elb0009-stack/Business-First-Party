THE DETAIL

What our plan
will deliver.

Select a priority to read the policy commitments.

01

BUSINESS & TAX

Let enterprise thrive.

Small businesses are essential to Australia's prosperity. We will reduce unnecessary compliance, simplify regulation and pursue a tax system that rewards work, investment and initiative.

  • Review regulations that create unnecessary costs for small businesses.
  • Support lower, simpler taxes that encourage investment and employment.
  • Strengthen competition so consumers and businesses have more choice.
02

ENERGY & ENVIRONMENT

Practical progress, not economic disruption.

Climate change and environmental protection matter. The transition must also protect jobs, maintain reliability and keep energy affordable.

  • Encourage research and investment in lower-emissions technologies.
  • Maintain a reliable and diverse energy supply.
  • Work with industry on economically sustainable environmental improvements.
03

JOBS & SKILLS

Make work and opportunity grow.

A strong economy creates pathways into employment and gives people the skills to adapt.

  • Expand access to apprenticeships, vocational education and work-based learning.
  • Remove barriers that prevent businesses from hiring and growing.
  • Support skilled migration where it addresses genuine workforce needs.
04

FAMILIES & CHOICE

Trust people to shape their future.

Families and individuals should have meaningful choices. We support focused government, responsible spending and personal initiative.

  • Support funding for both public and private schools.
  • Give parents and students greater choice in education.
  • Promote responsible budgeting and value for taxpayers.
